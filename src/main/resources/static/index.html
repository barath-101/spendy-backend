About Spendy

Spendy is a modern expense tracking application built with cutting-edge technology to help you take control of your finances. Our mission is to make financial management simple, intuitive, and accessible to everyone.

Built with Spring Boot, PostgreSQL, and modern web technologies, Spendy offers a robust and scalable solution for personal finance management.
